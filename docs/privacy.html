Privacy Policy for eTribunals

App: eTribunals (Android package com.zrh.etribunals.etribunals)
Last updated: 13 October 2026

eTribunals is an independent companion app for public Indian tribunal records. It is not a government website, not a court, and not legal advice. This policy explains what data the app uses, what stays on your device, and who else may receive data when you use certain features.

1. Who we are

eTribunals is published as an independent Android app. We do not operate a user-account server for this app. Questions: use Report a problem in the app’s More tab.

2. No account

You do not create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or government ID to use the app.

3. Data stored on your device

The following stays on the phone (or tablet) unless you delete it or uninstall the app:

  • Cases you follow (bookmarks), including case numbers, titles, parties, listing dates and links you saved
  • Recently viewed cases
  • PDFs or files you choose to save from the in-app viewer
  • Reminder on/off preference and local notification schedules
  • Onboarding completion and similar app settings

This data is not synced to an eTribunals cloud account. Uninstalling the app, or using in-app actions such as unfollow all / delete saved PDFs, removes that local data (files already copied to your Downloads folder may remain until you delete them).

4. Internet and public tribunal sites

When you search cases, open cause lists, display boards, orders or judgements, the app requests public pages and files from official tribunal websites (for example NCLAT, NCLT, NCDRC, ITAT, DRT and other listed forums). Those sites may see your device’s IP address, the URL requested, and standard browser-style headers, as with any website visit. We do not control those sites’ cookies, logs or policies.

5. AI Summarize (Google Gemini)

If you tap AI Summarize (or ask a follow-up about a summary), the app sends the open PDF or text taken from it to Google Gemini (Google LLC) so the app can list held points, directions and a short answer. We do not collect a Gemini API key from you. Google processes that content under Google’s Gemini API / generative AI terms. Do not use AI Summarize on a document you are not allowed to send to a third-party service.

6. Google Play, Firebase and device services

The app is distributed through Google Play and initialises Firebase (Google). Google Play services and Firebase may process device and app identifiers, crash or diagnostics information, and approximate country/language as described in Google’s privacy policy, in order to run the store, updates, and basic analytics or crash reporting. We do not sell this data.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications and keep reminders on, this device can show local alerts before a saved listing date (for example the evening before and the morning of the date). That is not a court notice. Android may show a permission prompt. You can turn reminders off in More, or disable notifications in system settings.

8. Who we share data with

  • Official tribunal websites — when you load their public records (see section 4)
  • Google LLC — Gemini when you use AI Summarize; Play / Firebase as in section 6

We do not sell personal information. We do not share followed-case lists with advertisers.

9. Children

eTribunals is for adults following tribunal records. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country).

10. Your choices

  • Do not tap AI Summarize if you do not want a PDF sent to Google
  • Turn off hearing reminders in More
  • Unfollow cases or delete saved PDFs in More
  • Clear app storage or uninstall in Android settings

11. Data safety summary

We do not collect a user profile on our own servers. Data that leaves the device is mainly: (a) requests to public court websites you choose to open, (b) document content you send to Gemini for a summary, and (c) standard Play/Firebase/device traffic needed to install and run the app.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

Use More → Report a problem in the app. Please do not send confidential case files by email unless you intend us to read them to debug a fault.
