FULL DESCRIPTION
eTribunals is an independent companion for public Indian tribunal records. It is not a government app and not legal advice.

Search case status, cause lists, orders and judgements from NCLAT, NCLT, UPREAT, NGT, RCT, DRT, CAT, ITAT, CESTAT, TDSAT, APTEL, SAT, AFT, NCDRC, ATFP and CGIT — the way those sites publish them.

Follow a matter on this phone. See followed hearings on Today. Save a PDF and open it in the app. Tap AI Summarize for held points and operative directions, then ask a short follow-up about that order.

Always verify on the official tribunal website before you file, pay a fee, or rely on a date.
